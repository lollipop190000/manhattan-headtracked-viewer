# 0004: Modes and story

- Status: Proposed
- Proposed by: @SWTY7
- Affects: design, world, audio, character
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Supersedes: none

## Question

What do players do, and how much story do we tell?

## Options

### A. Arcade only

Time trial and free swing. No story. Cheapest and the most direct way to test the swing.

### B. Arcade plus a light framing story

Same modes, with a thin frame (for example: the city has lost power and you restore beacons by swinging to rooftops, or you deliver packages across a blocked city). Gives each run a reason, costs a few sentences and a few UI cards, not cutscenes.

### C. Mission campaign

A sequence of missions with dialogue and unique goals. Highest cost; needs writing, voice or text, mission tools and much more level work.

## Recommendation

A for M1 and M2 to prove the fun, then B for M4. Pick the framing story after M1 playtests, when we know what the swing is good at (speed and height suggest delivery or beacons; precision suggests rescue). Story ideas are welcome as PRs against this file, one section per idea.

## Story seeds (not decisions)

- **Blackout:** every rooftop beacon must be re-lit before the timer ends.
- **Courier:** deliver items across the city; later items are fragile, so release gently.
- **Window:** the player is a camera operator filming a hero; the screen is the window of their apartment. Fits the head-tracked window idea (more novel; needs design work).
