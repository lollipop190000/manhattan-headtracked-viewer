## Change and validity

Core residency/desired changes invalidate their original/current Skyline dependencies. Group versions freeze before preparation's first yield; later changes remain queued. Only affected Skyline groups clear, compare and build primitive masks. Unaffected masks and their masked counts remain committed, including through other groups' cancellation and unloading. Source registration/removal, reload, Surface changes and origin rebases invalidate dependencies; Surface and common-origin changes conservatively invalidate all resident groups. Code that directly mutates an idle resident's renderer, mesh or material must call `ownership.Invalidate(root)` (or global `Invalidate()`). Loaded shared geometry is immutable, as before.

No material eligibility or geometry rule changes. Core coverage truth and material contracts are **not** cached across frames: all active Core providers are checked during preparation and again in the no-yield atomic publication. A changed provider discovered during reconciliation expands the affected groups, including unrelated groups whose masks would otherwise be retained. Every Skyline part's transform, material coverage, origin relationship and previously published mesh pointer is revalidated, including unchanged groups. Failures restore full fallback and discard versions. Cleanup/restoration still precedes provider release; partial exact ordinals and complete validated catalog replacement keep their existing semantics.
